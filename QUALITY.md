# Portfolio quality review

## Verified

- Home and three case-study pages checked at 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 480, 390, and 360 pixels: 28 checks, no horizontal page overflow.
- Mobile navigation opens and closes, closes after link selection, and responds to Escape.
- Keyboard skip link, visible focus treatment, section anchors, process accordions, and Design/Develop hero controls work.
- Light/dark preference persists after reload. Both themes were visually inspected.
- Reduced-motion preference disables entrance, hover, and scroll animations.
- All case studies contain the 14 requested sections and descriptive page titles.
- Automated browser runs reported no JavaScript or console errors.
- The layout was checked with a 200% root font-size setting at a 720-pixel viewport. This is a limited enlargement check, not a complete assistive-technology audit.
- WCAG 2 A/AA and WCAG 2.1 AA automated axe checks were run on all four pages in both themes. Contrast issues found in hero-control metadata were corrected and checked again.
- Production build generates individual HTML entry points, route-specific metadata, canonical URLs, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, and a favicon. Fonts are served locally.

Their interface visuals are labeled as illustrative concepts, and proposed UX considerations are distinguished from documented capabilities.
